The DO-HEALTH trial found a small but measurable slowing of epigenetic aging clocks over three years. Effect size is modest; the safety profile is well characterised.

How to read this report

This is not medical advice. Cellscript Bio reports research associations for the methylation markers found in your sample, labelled by how much evidence supports each one. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or predict disease. A Robust label means an association has replicated across independent cohorts; Speculative means it rests on small or inconsistent studies and may not hold. Clocks are only reported for the tissue they were trained on. Your file is processed on our servers, is not shared with any third party, and is deleted after processing.
